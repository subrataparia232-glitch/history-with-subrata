🇪🇺 Modern European History

RenaissanceRevival of classical learning and culture in Europe, beginning in Italy.
ReformationA major religious movement that challenged the authority and practices of the Roman Catholic Church.
Geographical DiscoveriesEuropean maritime exploration expanded global trade, empires and cultural contacts.
EnlightenmentAn intellectual movement emphasizing reason, liberty, natural rights and critical inquiry.
French RevolutionThe revolution of 1789 transformed France and spread ideas of citizenship and political equality.
Industrial RevolutionMechanisation and factory production transformed economies and societies.
Unification of Italy & GermanyNationalist movements created unified nation-states in the nineteenth century.
World War IThe global conflict of 1914–1918 reshaped Europe and international politics.
Russian RevolutionThe 1917 revolutions ended Tsarist rule and led to Bolshevik power.
World War IIThe 1939–1945 conflict transformed the international order and Europe.

🇮🇳 Modern Indian History

European Powers in IndiaPortuguese, Dutch, English and French trading powers established settlements and commercial networks.
British ExpansionThe East India Company expanded political control through wars, diplomacy and administrative policies.
Revolt of 1857A major uprising against Company rule that led to the transfer of power to the British Crown.
Social & Religious ReformReformers challenged social practices and promoted education and new religious ideas.
Indian National CongressFounded in 1885, it became a major platform of Indian nationalism.
Swadeshi MovementA movement encouraging indigenous goods and opposing the partition of Bengal.
Non-Cooperation MovementGandhi's movement of 1920–22 encouraged non-violent non-cooperation with colonial institutions.
Civil Disobedience MovementLaunched in 1930, including the Salt March, to challenge colonial laws.
Quit India MovementLaunched in 1942 with the call for an end to British rule.
Independence & PartitionIndia became independent in 1947 amid the Partition of British India.
